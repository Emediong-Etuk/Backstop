The problem
3:07AM
The borrower is asleep.
BCH falls. Her loan drops below 110% collateral.
It is liquidated, and all of her BCH collateral is gone.

Every guarantee is a require.

Safe on chainA keeper can only top up the borrower's own loan, within her limits.
Bitcoin Cash nativeCashScript, CashTokens, introspection, checkDataSig prices.
No frictionOne signature in, none during a rescue, one out. No server.

Honest limits
Known limitations

  

    
1
Demo oracle key on chipnetReal General Protocols message format; mainnet would use General Protocols' key.

    
2
A stale price means no rescue…but no liquidation either: Paryon liquidates on the same price.

    
3
A crash faster than any keeper, or within the cooldownThe stability pool can still liquidate first. Backstop reduces the risk, it does not remove it.

    
4
Redemptions are out of scope · one loan per vault
